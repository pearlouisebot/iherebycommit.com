Privacy Policy

Effective: October 7, 2026

I Hereby Commit is a project of Hatch Labs Holdings LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("Hatch Labs," "we," "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you apply at iherebycommit.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Applying isn't joining the study. Before the study begins, you'll get full details and a separate consent form, and your individual answers become study data only if you agree then.
  • We may publish aggregate statistics about who applied — never anything that identifies you.
  • We never sell your information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.
  • We may email you about other apps and products built by Hatch Labs. You can unsubscribe anytime.
  • Scientists only ever receive de-identified, anonymized data — never your name or contact details.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

What we collect

From you: your name; date of birth; city; whether you're applying as a single or as a couple; if you apply as a couple, your relationship stage and your partner's name and email; whether you have or want children; your email address; your mobile number if you give us one; and the social profile links you choose to share (LinkedIn, and optionally Instagram, X, and TikTok).

Automatically: your browser type, the page that referred you to us, which headline you were viewing when you applied, and a one-way scrambled ("hashed") version of your IP address that we use only to block spam. We don't currently use analytics, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking pixels. If that changes, we'll update this policy first.

How we use it

  • To review applications and contact you about Cohort 01 and the study.
  • To select and balance Cohort 01.
  • To send you text messages about the study, only if you opted in.
  • To analyze who is applying and to publish aggregate, de-identified statistics and findings about our applicant pool — for example, how many people applied as couples, which cities they're in, or what they say they want. We never publish anything that identifies an individual.
  • To tell you by email about other apps, products, and early-access invitations from Hatch Labs. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing from marketing doesn't remove you from the study list unless you ask us to.
  • To protect the site from bots and abuse.

Research, analysis and publishing

We analyze applications to plan and select our cohorts, and we may publish aggregate, de-identified statistics and findings drawn from them — in articles, press, presentations or research write-ups. Published results never include your name, contact details, social profiles, or anything else that identifies you.

By applying, you agree that your information may be analyzed and published in de-identified form, as described in our Terms. Using your individual answers as data in the study itself is different: that happens only after you receive full details and give separate informed consent. Scientists we work with receive only de-identified, anonymized data. You can ask us to stop using your information at any time by emailing contact@hatchlabs.com.

Your partner's information

If you apply as a couple, you give us your partner's name and email. We use it to connect your application with theirs and to contact your partner about the study: an invitation when it opens, and occasional reminders or updates about joining. Every email we send them includes an unsubscribe link, and we'll stop as soon as they ask. We will never use your partner's information to market other products, and we never sell or share it. Please share their details only if your partner is comfortable with that. Your partner can ask us to delete their information at any time by emailing contact@hatchlabs.com.

Your social profiles

We collect links to public profiles so we can confirm applicants are real people when Cohort 01 opens. We never post to your accounts or access anything beyond the public profile you link.

Text messages

If you opt in, we'll send occasional texts about the study through our messaging provider, Twilio. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of applying, and you don't need to give us a mobile number at all. We will not send you marketing texts about other Hatch Labs products unless you separately agree to receive them. We never share your mobile number or text-message consent with anyone for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run this site and our communications, and who may use it only on our behalf:

  • Supabase (database hosting, in the United States)
  • Cloudflare (security and bot protection)
  • GitHub (website hosting)
  • Twilio (text messages)
  • Twilio SendGrid or Mailchimp (email)

We share only de-identified, anonymized data with scientists, as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it. If Hatch Labs is reorganized or acquired, your information may transfer to the successor, which will be bound by this policy. We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you remain on our list and it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, including future Hatch Labs studies and products, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. When it's no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it. We may keep a minimal record of an opt-out or deletion request so we can keep honoring it. If you join the study, the study's consent terms will also govern your study data.

Security

Your information is encrypted in transit and stored in an access-restricted database. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can see it.

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to protect this site from spam and abuse. It may collect information about your device and browser for that purpose, and its use is subject to Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your information, get a copy of it, stop marketing emails, or remove yourself from the list by emailing contact@hatchlabs.com. We'll respond within 45 days. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our response, and we'll answer your appeal within 60 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under state law; we honor these requests from everyone, wherever you live. We won't treat you differently for making a request.

Age

This site is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we'll delete it.

Where your data is stored

In the United States.

Other Hatch Labs apps

Other apps and products from Hatch Labs have their own terms and privacy policies, which you'll be asked to accept when you sign up for them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change materially affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll email you before it takes effect.
