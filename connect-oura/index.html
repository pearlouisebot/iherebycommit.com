Connect Oura

Connect your Oura Ring to see your own sleep, readiness and activity data here, by date.

What we access

  • Your Oura profile and email (personal, email), to confirm it's you.
  • Daily sleep, readiness and activity summaries (daily), heart rate (heartrate), workouts and sessions (workout, session), tags (tag) and blood oxygen (spo2).
  • Stress (stress), heart health (heart_health) and ring details (ring_configuration).
  • You can switch any of these off on Oura's screen.

How we use it

  • We copy the last 30 days when you connect and show them to you as is. Your Oura tokens stay on our server and are encrypted.
  • We never run AI on your Oura data, and we never sell it.
  • Using it for research or matching needs your separate yes below. Both start off.
  • You can disconnect at any time. We revoke access and delete everything we copied.
