1. Who can use I Hereby Commit

You must be at least 18 years old. No exceptions.

2. What is not allowed

Don't use the site or our services to:

  • Break the law or help anyone else break it.
  • Post sexually explicit content in your profile, photos, or application.
  • Share, request, or promote child sexual exploitation or abuse of any kind.
  • Threaten, promote, or commit sexual violence.
  • Share non-consensual content, including intimate images of anyone without their consent.
  • Facilitate human trafficking or sex trafficking, including sexual exploitation.
  • Solicit anyone, including offering or asking for sex for money or anything else of value.
  • Run scams, romance fraud, or any attempt to defraud other members or us.
  • Aid, abet, or encourage abuse or harassment of anyone.

3. Fair use of the site

Don't submit fake or automated applications, apply on someone else's behalf, scrape the site, or interfere with how it works.

Don't use mutual friends, blocking, or any other feature to track, harass, or learn about someone else.

4. Enforcement

If you break this policy, we'll remove your application and account.

We may preserve data related to the violation.

We may report you to the authorities. We report suspected child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

For how we handle law enforcement requests, see Terms, section 6a.

5. How to report

To report a member or content that breaks this policy, email contact@hatchlabs.com. Tell us who or what you're reporting and why. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 first.
