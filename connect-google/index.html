I HEREBY COMMIT
Reviewer / demo mode

Find mutual friends

I Hereby Commit is a research study on what makes relationships last. Connecting Google lets you see which people you know are also in the study (mutual friends), and lets you block people you never want to be matched with or shown to.

What we access

  • Your Google account email (openid, email): to confirm it is you.
  • Email addresses and phone numbers in your Google Contacts (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.readonly).
  • Email addresses and phone numbers in your "Other contacts" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.other.readonly), such as people you've emailed but never saved.

How we use it

  • We read your contacts one time. Each email and phone number is turned into a one-way code before anything is saved. We never save names.
  • Mutual friends: the codes are matched against other members who also connected, so you see people you have in common.
  • Block someone: your own contacts' emails and phone numbers are sent back to this page only, so you can pick someone to block. They are not stored.
  • Never used for research or advertising, never sold, and we never contact anyone in your contacts. We don't read your Gmail, and we revoke Google access right after the one-time read.
  • Disconnect at any time and we delete what we imported.

I Hereby Commit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See our Privacy Policy.

Demo mode: no application is needed. A clearly marked demo applicant is created for your Google account on our test database.
