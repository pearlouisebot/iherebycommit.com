Connect Google

I Hereby Commit is a research study on what makes relationships last. Connecting Google lets us show you which of your friends are also in the study (mutual friends), and lets you block people you never want to be matched with.

What we access

  • Your Google account email (openid, email): to confirm it is you.
  • Email addresses and phone numbers in your Google Contacts (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.readonly).
  • Email addresses and phone numbers in your "Other contacts" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.other.readonly).

How we use it

  • We read your contacts one time. Each email and phone number is turned into a one-way code before anything is saved. We never save names.
  • The codes are used only to show you mutual friends in the study and to apply your blocks. They are never used for research, advertising or sold, and we never contact anyone in your contacts.
  • We don't read your Gmail. We revoke Google access right after the one-time read.
  • You can disconnect at any time, and we delete what we imported.

I Hereby Commit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
