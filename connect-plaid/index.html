Connect your bank

Link a bank or card account through Plaid. We show you what we saved, and you can disconnect at any time.

What we access

  • Your account names, the last 4 digits and account types, so you can see which accounts are linked.
  • Your last 30 days of transactions, which we total by spending category. We don't keep the individual transactions.
  • You sign in on Plaid's screen. We never see your bank password.

How we use it

  • Your Plaid access token stays on our server and is encrypted.
  • We never run AI on your financial data, and we never sell it.
  • Disconnect removes the link at Plaid and deletes everything we saved.
