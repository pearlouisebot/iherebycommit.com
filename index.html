WHAT YOU THINK SCIENCE KNOWS
MYTH
Couples last when they speak each other's love language.

MYTH
Introverts belong with introverts. Or with extroverts.
It barely matters.³
Personality similarity explains less than half a percent of how satisfied married couples are.
MYTH
The right questionnaire can find your match.
Chemistry can't be predicted on paper.⁴
Given 100+ traits and preferences, machine learning could predict who's generally desirable — but not which two people would click.
