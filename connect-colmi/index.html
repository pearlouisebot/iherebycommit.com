Connect Colmi ring

Connect your Colmi smart ring (R02, R06, R10 and similar) to see your own steps, heart rate and sleep here, by date.

What we access

  • Your browser talks to the ring directly over Bluetooth. No Colmi or QRing account and no password is involved.
  • We read the ring’s name and battery, set its clock to your computer’s time, and read the last 7 days of steps, heart rate, sleep, blood oxygen, stress, HRV and temperature.

How we use it

  • We save one summary per day (steps, calories, distance, heart rate, sleep, blood oxygen, stress, HRV and temperature) plus the individual readings and show them to you as is.
  • We never run AI on your ring data, and we never sell it.
  • Using it for research or matching needs your separate yes below. Both start off.
  • You can disconnect at any time. We delete everything we saved.
Before you start: the QRing app must be disconnected first. Close QRing on your phone (or turn your phone’s Bluetooth off) so the ring is free, keep the ring on your finger and charged, and keep it near your computer.

Open this page in Chrome on a computer or Android phone.

Health monitoring on the ring

Other health monitoring

The ring only records heart rate while you wear it. Readings appear here after it has been on your finger for a while and you sync again.

New ring? Set it up first. Turn on Automatic heart rate (the switch above), wear the ring for an hour, then tap Connect ring again to sync.
