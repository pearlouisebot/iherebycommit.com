Data Deletion Instructions

Effective: October 8, 2026

This page explains how to delete the information I Hereby Commit (iherebycommit.com, operated by Hatch Labs Holdings LLC) holds about you, including data from Facebook, Google, Oura, Apple Health, or Plaid if you connected them. Some of these connections may not be available to everyone yet; the steps for each one apply if you connected it. You don't need an account or a password to ask. Questions: contact@hatchlabs.com.

  • Facebook

Delete everything

Email contact@hatchlabs.com from the address you applied with, with the subject "Delete my data". We'll confirm when it's done. We delete your application and any data from connected accounts. Connected-account data is deleted within 72 hours; we'll complete the rest of your request within 45 days, and usually much sooner.

Facebook

If you connected Facebook to find friends in the study, you can delete that data any of these ways:

  • Remove our app in Facebook. In Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, choose I Hereby Commit, and tap Remove. Facebook notifies us, and we automatically delete the Facebook data we hold for you.
  • Send a deletion request through Facebook. On that same Apps and websites screen, request that I Hereby Commit delete your data. We delete it automatically, and Facebook gives you a confirmation code and a link where you can check the status of your request.
  • Disconnect on our site using Disconnect next to Facebook.
  • Email us at contact@hatchlabs.com with the subject "Delete my Facebook data". We delete it within 72 hours.

What gets deleted: your Facebook ID (which we store only as a one-way code) and your connections to Facebook friends who also joined. We never stored your name, photo, email, or posts from Facebook.

Google

  • Disconnect on our site using Disconnect next to Google.
  • Email us at contact@hatchlabs.com with the subject "Delete my Google data". We delete it within 72 hours.

What gets deleted: the one-way codes made from your contacts' email addresses and phone numbers, your Google account ID, and the connections created from your import. We never stored names or raw contact details.

You can also remove our access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections. We already give up access right after each one-time import, so removing it there doesn't delete what we imported. Use Disconnect or email us for that.

Oura

  • Disconnect on our site using Disconnect next to Oura. We stop collecting and using your Oura data right away.
  • In Oura, remove I Hereby Commit from the connected or authorized apps in your Oura account.
  • Email us at contact@hatchlabs.com with the subject "Delete my Oura data".

We delete all of your Oura data within 72 hours of a deletion request or of you deleting your application.

Apple Health

Apple Health can only be connected in our iPhone app.

  • Disconnect in our iPhone app using Disconnect next to Apple Health. We stop receiving your Apple Health data right away and delete what we received.
  • On your iPhone, go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → I Hereby Commit and tap Turn Off All (on some iOS versions it's Settings → Apps → Health → Data Access & Devices). This stops new data reaching us, but iOS doesn't tell us, so it doesn't delete what we already have. Use Disconnect or email us for that.
  • Email us at contact@hatchlabs.com with the subject "Delete my Apple Health data".

We delete all of your Apple Health data within 72 hours of a deletion request or of you deleting your application. Nothing in Apple Health on your iPhone is changed or deleted.

Bank account (Plaid)

  • Disconnect on our site using Disconnect next to your bank. We remove our access through Plaid and delete the financial data we stored.
  • Email us at contact@hatchlabs.com with the subject "Delete my bank data". We delete it within 72 hours.
  • You can also manage connections made through Plaid at my.plaid.com.

If someone listed you as their partner

Use the unsubscribe link in the email we sent you, or email contact@hatchlabs.com with the subject "Remove me" and the address that received our email. We'll delete your name and email from their application and stop contacting you.

What we may keep

  • A minimal record that you asked, so we can keep honoring your request.
  • If another member has blocked you, a one-way code of your email or phone number (nothing else), so their block stays in place if you apply again. You can ask us to remove it.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's encrypted backups, for a short time until they're overwritten.
