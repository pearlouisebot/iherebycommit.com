Connect Fitbit

Connect your Fitbit (through your Google account) to see your own daily steps, activity and fitness data here, by date.

Since September 2026, Fitbit data is shared through Google. You’ll sign in with the Google account your Fitbit uses.

What we access

  • Your Google account email (openid, email), to confirm it’s you.
  • Daily activity and fitness, health measurements and sleep from Fitbit, read-only, through the Google Health API (activity_and_fitness, health_metrics_and_measurements, sleep).

How we use it

  • We copy the last 30 days when you connect and show them to you as is. Your Fitbit tokens stay on our server and are encrypted.
  • We never run AI on your Fitbit data, and we never sell it.
  • Using it for research or matching needs your separate yes below. Both start off.
  • You can disconnect at any time. We revoke access and delete everything we copied.
