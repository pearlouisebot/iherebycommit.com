Connect Apple Health

Share a summary of your Apple Health data: steps, sleep, heart and workouts, by day, for the last 90 days.

How to do it on your iPhone

  1. Open the Health app. (Do this on your iPhone.)
  2. Tap your picture or initials at the top right. (If you don't see it, tap Summary at the bottom first.)
  3. Scroll all the way down and tap Export All Health Data.
  4. Tap Export.
  5. Wait. It can take a few minutes. Keep the Health app open.
  6. When the share menu appears, tap Save to Files, pick On My iPhone, and tap Save.
  7. Come back to this page in Safari on the same iPhone.
  8. Tap Upload below, then tap Browse and choose Export.zip.

What we keep

  • Your iPhone reads the file right here on this page. The file itself is never uploaded.
  • We only save one line per day, for the last 90 days or all your history (you choose): steps, hours asleep, resting heart rate, HRV, active calories, workouts (how many, minutes, type), VO2 max and blood oxygen.
  • We never run AI on your health data, and we never sell it.
  • Using it for research or matching needs your separate yes below. Both start off.
  • You can delete it at any time.
