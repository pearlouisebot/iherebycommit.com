Upload your Strava export

Share a summary of your workouts from the archive Strava lets you download: date, type, time, distance, climb and heart rate.

How to get your archive

  1. On strava.com (in a browser, not the app), go to Settings, then My Account.
  2. Under Download or Delete Your Account, tap Get Started, then Request your archive.
  3. Strava emails you a link to a .zip, usually within a few hours. It holds activities.csv plus your workout files (.fit, .gpx, .tcx, often ending in .gz).
  4. Download the zip and save it to Files (iPhone) or Downloads.
  5. Come back here, tap Upload and pick the .zip, or just the activities.csv inside it.

What we read and keep

  • Your browser reads the archive right here on this page. Only activities.csv is read. The archive itself is never uploaded.
  • For each activity we keep only: date and time, type, moving and elapsed time, distance, elevation gain, average and max heart rate, and calories and relative effort if Strava included them.
  • We never open, upload or store your GPS tracks, routes or coordinates. Your .fit, .gpx and .tcx files are skipped. So are activity names, descriptions, private notes, gear, photos and your profile.
  • We never run AI on this data, and we never sell it.
  • Using it for research or matching needs your separate yes below. Both start off.
  • Uploading again only adds activities we don't have yet. Nothing already saved is removed. You can delete it all at any time with the link at the bottom of this page.
