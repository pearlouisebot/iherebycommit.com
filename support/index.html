Membership & billing

Founding Member membership is $99 per year, billed through Stripe.

  • Manage your subscription. Use the link in your Stripe receipt or invoice email, or email us from the address you used to join and we'll send you a customer portal link.
  • Cancel. Email contact@hatchlabs.com with the subject "Cancel membership". We'll confirm when it's done.
  • Refunds. To request a refund, email contact@hatchlabs.com with the subject "Refund request" and the email you used at checkout.

Texts
