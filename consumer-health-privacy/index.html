Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

Effective: October 17, 2026

This notice explains how Hatch Labs Holdings LLC ("Hatch Labs," "we," "us"), which runs I Hereby Commit, a relationship research study and matchmaking membership, handles consumer health data. It is written to meet consumer health data laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act, and we give the rights below to everyone, wherever you live. It supplements our Privacy Policy; if they differ, this notice controls for consumer health data.

Consumer health data we collect

  • Oura Ring data, only if you connect Oura: daily sleep, readiness, activity, stress, and blood oxygen (SpO2) summaries; heart rate; heart health (such as cardiovascular age and VO2 max estimates); workouts; sessions; tags; ring settings; basic personal info (age, sex, height, weight); and the email address on your Oura account.
  • Apple Health data, only if you connect Apple Health in our iPhone app, as listed in the Privacy Policy.
  • Other wearables or health apps we may offer later, only if you connect them. We'll list the data types on the consent screen and in our Privacy Policy before you connect.
  • Information you give us that may reveal something about your health, such as your height or answers to optional application or study questions.

Sources. You, and the wearable or health service you choose to connect (for example, Oura or Apple Health on your iPhone). Our access to connected services is read-only. We don't use geofencing around health-care locations, and we don't collect location data from wearables.

Why we collect and use it

We collect consumer health data only with your consent, and each use has its own separate opt-in:

  • To show you your own summaries.
  • To improve your match recommendations. Other members never see your health data; it only informs the recommendations we make.
  • In de-identified, aggregated form, for research on links between sleep, health, and relationships. Using your individual data in a study needs your separate informed consent.
  • To keep connections working and secure, and to comply with the law.

We never sell consumer health data, never use it for advertising or marketing, never use it to train, fine-tune, or feed artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models, and never use it for credit, insurance, or employment decisions.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers (processors) that process it only on our behalf under contract: Supabase (database hosting) and Cloudflare (hosting and security).
  • People you choose, such as your partner, only if you expressly choose to share with them.
  • Researchers, only in de-identified, aggregated form that can't identify you, and for Oura data only as our agreement with Oura permits.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data. We share it in any other way only with your separate consent.

How long we keep it

Oura data is kept until you disconnect Oura or delete your account, and is deleted when you do. Apple Health data is kept as described in our Privacy Policy. You can ask us to delete any consumer health data at any time; see Data deletion instructions.

Your rights

  • Confirm and access: whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, a copy of it, and a list of every third party and affiliate we've shared it with, with their contact information.
  • Withdraw consent to collection or sharing at any time, by disconnecting the service or emailing us.
  • Delete your consumer health data, including from our service providers.
  • Appeal if we decline your request.
  • No discrimination for using these rights.

How to make a request. Email contact@hatchlabs.com with the subject "Consumer health data request," or use Disconnect next to a connected service on our site. We'll verify your request, respond within 45 days (we may extend once by 45 days if reasonably necessary and will tell you), and won't charge you. To appeal a decision, reply to our response with the subject "Appeal"; we'll answer within 45 days. If your appeal is denied and you live in Washington, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update the effective date above. We'll ask for your consent before collecting or sharing consumer health data in a new way or for a new purpose.
